Brief
─────
A small editorial illustration of a completed verse line accompanied by a quiet
mark of confirmation — a soft sage check or laurel half-wreath. The reader's
role here is recall under their own steam — typing or speaking the verse cold,
no prompts.

Composition
- A horizontal typographic line (abstracted, not readable) with a small
  check / wreath floating to its right.
- The check sits inside a circle that echoes the icon discs in the live
  component, so the illustration reads as a "moment", not a UI screenshot.
- Centered in a square frame.

Style / mood
- Quiet confidence. Not a trophy moment — that comes at Master.
- Slight upward float on the check, suggesting "settled into place".
- Soft sage halo at very low opacity around the check (no hard outline).

Color guidance (semantic tokens)
- Type line: on-surface-variant.
- Check / wreath: secondary (sage) at full strength; halo secondary-container
  @ ~30%.
- Avoid pure success-green (#66BB6A) — that's the in-app demo feedback color
  for "correct answer." Prove is calmer.
- No primary, no gold here. This step is intentionally restrained.

Dimensions / format
- Square (1:1). 480×480 PNG or SVG. Transparent background.

Sourcing options
1. Custom SVG (preferred).
2. Lucide ShieldCheck enlarged + warm-tinted as a starting point.
3. AI-generated stylized illustration — owner edits.

Swap pattern: see study.txt.
