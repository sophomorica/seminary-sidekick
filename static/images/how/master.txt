Brief
─────
A small editorial illustration of a verse line haloed in soft gold — the sealing
moment. This is the loop payoff and the one step where the sacred-gold palette
earns its place (mastery-eternal, per THEME.md mastery colors).

Composition
- A horizontal typographic line (abstracted, not readable) with a delicate gold
  starburst / sparkle group above it.
- Sparkles are small, varied in size, scattered (not a uniform pattern).
- Centered in a square frame.

Style / mood
- Reverent and quietly celebratory. The end of one pass through the loop and
  the beginning of the next.
- Slight ambient glow around the line — soft, no hard rays.

Color guidance (semantic tokens)
- Sparkles: mastery-eternal (#CCA72F, equal to premium-gold) — this IS the one
  place in How-it-works where gold is appropriate.
- Glow halo: premium-gold-light @ ~25% opacity.
- Type line: on-surface (a touch darker than the Prove step to read as "locked
  in").
- Keep the gold restrained — the illustration should still read as warm-paper,
  not Vegas.

Dimensions / format
- Square (1:1). 480×480 PNG or SVG. Transparent background.

Sourcing options
1. Custom SVG (preferred) — owns the brand sparkle vocabulary so it can be
   reused in the future "achievements" UI.
2. Lucide Sparkles enlarged + custom gold gradient — starting point only.
3. AI-generated stylized illustration matching THEME.md palette — owner edits.
