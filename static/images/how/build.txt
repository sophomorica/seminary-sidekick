Brief
─────
A small editorial illustration of word tiles forming a scripture line — the
"Scripture Builder" metaphor made visual. Some tiles fully snapped into a row,
one or two still floating into place. The reader's role here is active
construction — putting the verse back together.

Composition
- Three to five rectangular word tiles laid out in a tight horizontal row.
- One tile sits above the row, mid-drop, suggesting motion (just landing).
- Tiles read as soft pebble-shapes (rounded-2xl-ish corners), not Tetris cubes.
- Centered in a square frame, slight downward tilt for life.

Style / mood
- Tactile, calm, playful but not childish. The reader is "building" the verse.
- Soft tinted shadows beneath each tile (no outlines).
- Minimal text on tiles — abstracted to typographic blocks, no readable verse.

Color guidance (semantic tokens)
- Tile surfaces: surface-container-lowest with subtle on-surface marks.
- One tile (the "next" one) tinted secondary-container (sage) to signal the
  current target.
- Tile in motion casts a slightly larger shadow-floating for elevation.
- Avoid premium gold and brand primary here — primary is reserved for the loop
  payoff (Master) and primary CTAs.

Dimensions / format
- Square (1:1). 480×480 PNG or SVG. Background transparent.

Sourcing options
1. Custom SVG (preferred) — matches the Scripture Builder UI in the app.
2. Adapted from the Flutter Scripture Builder screen (screen-grab → flatten →
   simplify into illustration form).
3. AI-generated stylized illustration matching THEME.md palette — owner edits.
