Composition
-----------
A clean, stylized "screenshot" of the Sidekick AI journal / chat surface
from inside the Seminary Sidekick app. Not a real screen capture — a
designed mockup that reads as the app's voice. Shows three things in
the frame, top to bottom:

  1. A "Today" header chip with a small gold sparkle icon and a date
     line ("Wednesday, May 27"). Faint Sidekick wordmark on the right.

  2. A Sidekick AI prompt card (the hero element). Soft rounded
     rectangle (≈32px radius), surface-container-lowest white fill,
     premium-gold-light (#FFE088) left edge marker stripe ~4px wide.
     Title in serif italic: "Today's prompt." Body in sans-serif:
     a short, reverent reflection question tied to a doctrinal mastery
     scripture (suggested copy: "How does keeping the commandments
     show love for the Savior in your life this week?"). A "From John
     14:15" pill in book-nt indigo at the bottom of the card.

  3. A user reply bubble below (right-aligned, primary-fixed (#FFDBCF)
     fill, on-primary-container text), short two-line answer that
     trails off mid-thought with a soft caret — implying the journal
     is being actively written.

  4. A floating "Suggested goal" chip at the very bottom: small
     premium-gold (#CCA72F) outline, sparkle icon, label
     "Memorize John 14:15 by Friday."

Style
-----
- Editorial, calm, premium. Same brand voice as the rest of the site.
- Use the THEME.md palette: surface (#FFF8F6) background, on-surface
  (#221A17) text, premium-gold (#CCA72F) and premium-gold-light
  (#FFE088) as accents, book-nt (#5C6BC0) for the scripture pill.
- Merriweather for the prompt title and any italic moments. Inter for
  body, chips, labels. Match the type scale: ~headline-md for the
  prompt title, ~body-md for the prompt body, ~label-md for chips.
- Smooth-stones radii throughout (rounded-3xl on cards, rounded-full
  on chips). Tinted shadows only (not gray) — the "shadow-floating"
  recipe from app.css: 0 12px 40px rgb(34 26 23 / 0.04).
- A subtle premium-gold-light radial glow behind the card (already
  partly rendered in the Svelte component) so the screenshot still
  feels premium when dropped into the section.
- No device chrome / no phone frame. The component already provides
  a floating card frame; the screenshot itself should look like a
  flat UI extract, not a phone bezel.
